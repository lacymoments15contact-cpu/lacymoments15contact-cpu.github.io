Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

1. Data we access

When the account owner connects their TikTok account, the App receives an access token and basic profile information (such as the account's display name and open ID) through TikTok Login Kit. This is only used to upload videos to that same account and to check the status of those uploads.

2. Data we do not collect

The App does not collect data from other TikTok users or viewers. It does not read messages, followers' personal data or any other private information, and it does not use cookies or tracking for advertising.

3. How data is used

Access tokens are used only to publish the account owner's own videos and captions and to read the publishing status. Data is never sold, rented or shared for marketing purposes.

4. Storage and security

Access tokens are stored securely by the integration service used to connect the account and are only used for the purposes above. Video files are kept only as long as needed to upload them.

5. Deleting your data

The account owner can disconnect the App at any time in their TikTok settings under "Security" and "Manage app permissions". This revokes the App's access. Requests to delete any remaining data can be sent to the contact address below and will be handled within 30 days.

6. Children

The App is not directed to children and does not knowingly process data of children.
